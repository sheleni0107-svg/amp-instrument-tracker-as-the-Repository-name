Admin Guide: Deleting Records & Managing Clean Data

Simple instructions for AMP staff on maintaining real data, deleting test items, and exporting clean spreadsheets.

1

Deleting Individual Records

To delete any learner, instrument, or loan transaction, simply click the red Delete button in the rightmost column of any table. A confirmation box will appear to prevent accidental clicks.

2

Automatic Storage & Persistence

All additions, inline text edits, and record deletions are automatically saved into your browser's local memory (`localStorage`). When you refresh or reopen the page, your data remains up-to-date.

3

Exporting Fresh Excel Files

Whenever you want an offline backup or Google Sheets upload, click Download Excel (.XLSX) at the top right. It will generate a clean, 4-tab workbook containing only your active records.
