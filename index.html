System Administration & Sync Guide

Access Music Project (AMP) Asset Management Protocol

This application facilitates seamless instrument tracking across learner cohorts, maintaining complete synchronization between active hires, learner qualifications, and physical asset conditions.

1. Automated Status Synchronization (`syncWorksheets`)

  • When a loan log is marked as Active, the associated instrument asset automatically updates to On Hire.
  • Assets flagged as In Repair or Decommissioned retain their primary status to prevent accidental overwrite during active loan sync.
  • Marking a loan as Returned or Cancelled restores the corresponding instrument status to Available.

2. Loan Eligibility Guardrails

  • Loans should ideally be issued to learners with a QUALIFIED status to preserve instrument safety.
  • Only assets currently listed as Available are selectable in the New Loan issuance modal.

3. Data Persistence & Multi-Tab Excel Generation

  • All edits, additions, and deletions auto-save immediately to browser localStorage.
  • Clicking Export Multi-Tab Excel produces a structured .xlsx file containing four dedicated worksheets: Learners, Assets, Active Loans, and Executive Summary.
